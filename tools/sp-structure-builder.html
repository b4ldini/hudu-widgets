SharePoint Structure Planner

Sketch how you'd like your files organised after the move, and who should be able to see what. No SharePoint knowledge needed — every term is explained as you go, and the checker keeps your plan on track.
▶ How this works — please read before you start

First, the words you'll see. SharePoint (the system your files are moving to) has its own names for things:

  • Site — a separate area for one part of your organisation (Finance, HR, Operations…). Think of it as a filing cabinet with its own keyholders.
  • Library (short for document library) — a section inside a site, like a drawer in that cabinet. This is the level where "who can open it" is decided.
  • Folder — ordinary folders inside a library, just like the ones you have today. Anyone who can open the library can open all of its folders.
  • Group — a named list of people, e.g. "the finance team". Access is given to groups rather than to individuals one at a time, so someone joining or leaving is a single change.
  • Owners — the people responsible for a site, who decide who else is allowed in. Every site needs at least two, so there's always someone available.

What to do:

  1. Map your structure. Add a site for each broad area, libraries inside each site, and folders inside libraries for everyday filing.
  2. Add your people. In the People panel below the structure, type each person once — a name or an email address. You'll never need to type them again.
  3. Make your groups. In Access groups, click people's names to say who belongs in each group.
  4. Decide who can see what. Click details on a site to click its owners and tick which groups use it. If everyone on the site can see a library, leave it as "same as site"; if it's for a smaller circle, mark it restricted and tick the group(s) who should have it.
  5. Watch the health check on the right — it flags anything that would cause problems later, and tells you how to fix it.
  6. Send it back to us. When you're happy, use Copy as text and paste the whole thing into an email to your Active8 contact — replying to the message that sent you this link is perfect. It's a readable summary with a "plan data" code at the bottom that lets us load your design exactly as you built it. Please keep that code intact. (Prefer something shorter? Copy share code gives just the code on its own — pasting it into Load a plan recreates everything.) Your draft also saves automatically in this browser, so you can come back to it.

One rule this planner enforces: access is decided on whole libraries, never on individual folders. If one folder needs to be private (say, salaries within HR), it should become its own library — the planner will guide you when that happens.
