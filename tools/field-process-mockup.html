Field job scheduling, evidence & reporting — Autotask + Hudu connected MOCKUP · sample data

One app in the field — reporting becomes a by-product

Today, reporting is a separate chore done after the job — so it doesn't happen. In this model the technician works through the app, and every action automatically becomes the Autotask ticket note, the Hudu record and the report row. The tech cannot check out until the closeout gate is satisfied.

1

Schedule the job

Office builds the job from a template — phases & planned hours (same structure as the Job Scope Sheet).

→ Autotask ticket created
2

Tech checks in

Arrival timestamped on site. Clock starts — actual time is measured, not recalled.

→ ticket note
3

Work the phases

Start / complete each phase in the app. Evidence photo required per phase.

→ notes + time entries · → photos to Hudu
4

Credentials captured

New device or account passwords go into a structured form on the spot.

→ Hudu password entry (right company)
5

Problems flagged

Anything found on site is flagged with photo + note — it can't stay in the tech's head.

→ flagged note / follow-up ticket
6

Closeout gate

No check-out until phases are complete or flagged, evidence & credentials are in, and the customer has signed. Unfinished job? Reason required → reattendance ticket auto-raised.

→ closure or follow-up ticket
7

Reports build themselves

Job Log & Combined Report generated live — planned vs actual, variance, anomalies.

→ management dashboard

What this fixes

Problems not reported

Flagging is one tap with a photo — and the gate asks "any problems?" before checkout.

Jobs left incomplete silently

Can't check out with open phases unless a reason is given — which auto-raises the reattendance ticket.

Autotask not updated

Ticket, notes and time entries are generated from the tech's actions. Zero after-job admin.

Credentials missing from Hudu

Credential capture is a gate item — the job cannot close with credentials pending.

Evidence on demand

Photos are taken as work completes and filed to the right company automatically. No chasing.

True job costing

Timestamps give honest actual hours per phase — the variance data in the reporting pack, automatically.

Scheduler — the office view

Jobs are built from templates (Onboarding / Project) with the phase breakdown and planned hours — exactly the Job Scope Sheet, digitised. Saving a job creates the Autotask ticket automatically.

Why the Surface works for this
  • Location fed automatically — check-in/check-out are geo-stamped and the app confirms the device is at the scheduled site. Arrival, time on site, and departure need no honesty system.
  • Photos still mandatory — captured on the Surface camera (or synced from a phone), filed to the right Hudu company on the spot. A phase cannot complete without its evidence.
  • 4G = live sync — the office sees ticket notes and Hudu records the moment they happen; if signal drops, actions queue and sync when it returns.
  • Touchscreen sign-off — the tech hands the Surface to the customer to sign; the signature lands on the ticket.
  • Big screen, no excuses — the closeout gate is permanently on screen. The tech can always see exactly what stands between them and leaving site.

Closeout gate — required before leaving site

Demo script (2 minutes)
  1. Phase 4 is in progress — tap 📷 Take photo, then Complete. Note the phase can't complete without evidence.
  2. Start and complete phases 5 and 6 the same way.
  3. Tap 🔑 Add credential — the switch password goes straight to Hudu against Bright Law.
  4. Tap ⚠ Flag a problem — the faulty patch panel port is logged and a follow-up ticket is raised. Nothing stays in the tech's head.
  5. Capture the signature — the gate goes green and Check out unlocks.
  6. Open the Live Job Feed tab — everything you just did is already in Autotask and Hudu.

Also try Can't finish — a reason is required and a reattendance ticket is raised automatically. There is no way to leave site silently.
