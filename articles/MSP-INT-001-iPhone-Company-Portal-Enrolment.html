Article Overview

This guide walks you through setting up your personal iPhone so you can securely access your work apps and data — Outlook, Teams, OneDrive and more — using the Intune Company Portal app.

Enrolment registers your device with your organisation’s Microsoft 365 environment and typically takes 10–15 minutes. Worried about what work can see on a personal phone? The Your Privacy section explains exactly what your organisation can and cannot see.

Takes 10–15 minutes
Personal iPhone (BYOD)
iOS 14 or later
Outlook · Teams · OneDrive
⚠
Before you begin

Stay connected to Wi-Fi for the whole process and don’t pause for more than a few minutes between steps — the setup can time out and you’ll need to start again.

Preparation
What You’ll Need

Have these four things ready before you start — it keeps the whole process smooth and avoids the setup timing out partway through.

Requirement Details
📱 iPhone Running iOS 14 or later, connected to Wi-Fi
💼 Work account Your work email address and password (e.g. j.smith@yourcompany.com)
🍎 Apple ID Signed in to the App Store so you can download apps
🔐 MFA access Access to your usual sign-in approval method (Microsoft Authenticator, text message, or phone call)

2

Step 02 of 06
Sign In with Your Work Account
1

Open the Company Portal app.

2

Sign in with your work email address and password.

3

Approve the multi-factor authentication prompt if you receive one.

4

If asked to allow notifications, tap Allow — this is how the app tells you about anything that needs your attention later.

↓
3
Step 03 of 06
Begin Device Setup
1

On the How to set up your device (or Set up access) screen, review the steps shown and tap Get started / Begin.

2

If asked who owns the device, tap I own this device.

3

Safari will open and prompt you to download a configuration profile. Tap Allow.

4

When the download completes, tap Close.

↓
4
Step 04 of 06
Install the Management Profile
1

Open the Settings app and tap Profile Downloaded near the top. If you don’t see it, go to General > VPN & Device Management.

2

Tap Management Profile, then tap Install.

3

Enter your iPhone passcode if prompted, then tap Install again.

4

Read the remote management notice, tap Install once more, then tap Trust.

5

Wait for the profile to finish installing, then tap Done.

⚠
Passcode required

If your iPhone doesn’t currently have a passcode set, you’ll be asked to create one as part of enrolment. This is a security requirement for accessing work data.

↓
5
Step 05 of 06
Finish in the Company Portal App
1

Return to the Company Portal app and open Notifications.

2

Under pending requirements, tap the option to register your device if shown.

3

Wait a few minutes while your device receives its settings. You may be prompted to install required apps such as Microsoft Authenticator — tap Install (or Manage if it’s already installed) on each prompt.

↓
6
Step 06 of 06
Sign In to Your Work Apps
1

Install any work apps you need — Outlook, Teams, OneDrive — from the Company Portal app or the App Store.

2

Open an app and sign in with your work account.

3

The first sign-in checks that your device meets your organisation’s security requirements. If prompted, tap Check compliance, follow the instructions shown in Company Portal, then tap Retry.

Worth Knowing
Your Privacy — What Your Organisation Can and Can’t See
Personal Device, Personal Data

Enrolling a personal device is a common concern

Enrolment lets your organisation apply security settings and remove work data if needed — it does not give access to your personal life.

Your Organisation CANNOT See
Your personal life stays private
  • Personal texts, emails, photos or videos
  • Browsing history or app usage
  • Your location
  • Passwords, contacts or calendar (personal)
Your Organisation CAN See
Only what’s needed for security
  • Device model, name and serial number
  • Operating system version and compliance status
  • Names of installed apps (not their content)
  • Work email address and work data on the device
🔒
If your device is lost, or you leave the organisation

Only the work profile and work data are removed — your personal apps, photos and files are untouched.

If Something Goes Wrong
Troubleshooting

Common Problems & Fixes

Click any problem to expand the fix

Sign-in fails in Company Portal
Double-check you’re using your work email and password, not a personal Microsoft account. If your password has expired, reset it before enrolling.
“Profile Downloaded” doesn’t appear in Settings
Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. If nothing is there, repeat Step 3 — the profile download may not have completed.
Setup closed or timed out partway through
Reopen the Company Portal app and sign in again — it resumes from where you left off.
Outlook or Teams says the device isn’t compliant
Open Company Portal > Notifications and complete any pending requirements (e.g. set a passcode, update iOS). Then tap Retry in the app. Allow a few minutes for the status to refresh.
Error about Microsoft Authenticator when signing in
Authenticator must finish installing before work apps will sign in. Wait a few minutes after enrolment, then try again.
