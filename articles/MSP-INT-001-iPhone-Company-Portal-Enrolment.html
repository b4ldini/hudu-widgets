Article Overview

This guide walks you through setting up your personal phone — iPhone or Android — so you can securely access your work apps and data (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive and more) using the Intune Company Portal app.

Enrolment registers your device with your organisation’s Microsoft 365 environment and typically takes 10–15 minutes. Worried about what work can see on a personal phone? The Your Privacy section explains exactly what your organisation can and cannot see.

Takes 10–15 minutes
iPhone & iPad — iOS 14 or later
Android — Android 10 or later
Outlook · Teams · OneDrive
⚠
Before you begin

Stay connected to Wi-Fi for the whole process and don’t pause for more than a few minutes between steps — the setup can time out and you’ll need to start again.

1

If Something Goes Wrong · iPhone
Troubleshooting

Common Problems & Fixes — iPhone

Tap any problem to expand the fix

Sign-in fails in Company Portal
Double-check you’re using your work email and password, not a personal Microsoft account. If your password has expired, reset it before enrolling.
“Profile Downloaded” doesn’t appear in Settings
Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. If nothing is there, repeat Step 3 — the profile download may not have completed.
Setup closed or timed out partway through
Reopen the Company Portal app and sign in again — it resumes from where you left off.
Outlook or Teams says the device isn’t compliant
Open Company Portal > Notifications and complete any pending requirements (e.g. set a passcode, update iOS). Then tap Retry in the app. Allow a few minutes for the status to refresh.
Error about Microsoft Authenticator when signing in
Authenticator must finish installing before work apps will sign in. Wait a few minutes after enrolment, then try again.

1

💼
What is a “work profile”?
Android creates a completely separate, sealed-off area on your phone just for work apps and work files. Think of it as a locked drawer inside your phone: work things go in the drawer, and everything personal — your photos, WhatsApp, banking, browsing — stays outside it and is never visible to your organisation.

Let Your Phone Create the Work Profile
1

Your phone will now show Setting up your work profile. This usually takes 2–5 minutes — leave the phone alone and keep it on Wi-Fi.

2

The screen may flash, go blank, or return to your home screen partway through. That is normal — don’t press anything.

3

When it finishes you’ll see Work profile setup complete or a notification that your work profile is ready. Tap Continue / Next.

4

You’ll now have a second Company Portal icon with a small briefcase on it. From here on, always use the one with the briefcase.

⚠
Screen lock required

If your phone doesn’t have a PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint set, you’ll be asked to create one. This is a security requirement for accessing work data — the setup can’t finish without it.

💼 The briefcase badge — the one thing to remember
After setup you’ll see two copies of some apps. They look almost identical. The one with the little briefcase symbol in the corner is the work one — that’s the one connected to your work email and files. The plain one is your own personal app and is untouched.

If Something Goes Wrong · Android
Troubleshooting

Common Problems & Fixes — Android

Tap any problem to expand the fix

Sign-in fails in Company Portal
Double-check you’re using your work email and password, not a personal Microsoft account. If your password has expired, reset it before enrolling.
I’ve got two of the same app — which one do I use?
Always the one with the small briefcase symbol in the corner of the icon. That’s the work copy, connected to your work account. The plain one is your own personal app and stays completely separate.
“Can’t set up work profile” or the setup stops partway
Check Settings > About phone > Android version is 10 or later, make sure you’re on Wi-Fi with a good signal, then restart the phone and reopen Company Portal — it picks up where it left off. If it still fails, contact IT Support.
My work apps have gone grey / say “Work profile paused”
Android lets you pause work apps so they stop notifying you. Open your app list, go to the Work tab, and turn the Work apps switch back on (or tap the “Work profile paused” notification and tap Unpause). Nothing is lost while it’s paused.
I can’t find the work Play Store or my work apps
Swipe up on the home screen to open your full app list, then tap the Work tab at the top. Everything with a briefcase badge lives there, including the work Play Store.
Outlook or Teams says the device isn’t compliant
Open the badged Company Portal and complete anything listed under Update device settings (e.g. set a screen lock, update Android). Then tap Retry in the app and allow a few minutes for the status to refresh.
My phone asks me to set a PIN or fingerprint — do I have to?
Yes. A screen lock is a security requirement for holding work data on a phone, and enrolment can’t finish without one. Some phones ask for a separate lock just for the work profile — that’s fine and only applies to work apps.
Error about Microsoft Authenticator when signing in
Authenticator must finish installing before work apps will sign in. Wait a few minutes after enrolment, then try again.

Worth Knowing · iPhone & Android
Your Privacy — What Your Organisation Can and Can’t See
Personal Device, Personal Data

Enrolling a personal device is a common concern

Enrolment lets your organisation apply security settings and remove work data if needed — it does not give access to your personal life. This is true on both iPhone and Android.

Your Organisation CANNOT See
Your personal life stays private
  • Personal texts, emails, photos or videos
  • Browsing history or app usage
  • Your location
  • Passwords, contacts or calendar (personal)
Your Organisation CAN See
Only what’s needed for security
  • Device model, name and serial number
  • Operating system version and compliance status
  • Names of work apps installed (not their content)
  • Work email address and work data on the device
🔒
If your device is lost, or you leave the organisation

Only the work profile and work data are removed — your personal apps, photos and files are untouched.

On Android this is especially clear-cut: everything work-related lives inside the separate work profile, so removing it deletes the badged work apps and their data and leaves the personal side of your phone exactly as it was.
