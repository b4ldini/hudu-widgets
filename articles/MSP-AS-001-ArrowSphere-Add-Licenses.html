4
Set the end of month term — Ordering Options  Critical step

In the Items in your order section, click the ✎ edit (pencil) icon on the Microsoft line item. The Ordering Options panel slides out on the right.

Scroll to the Term end date section in the panel:

Ordering Options
Term end date
30/04/2027  (auto-calculated — do not change)
Full term ❌ Never use this. End date falls mid-month and causes billing misalignment.
Calendar Month  ← select this Adding seats to an existing SKU — aligns end date to last day of month.
Coterminate Adding a brand new SKU to a customer who already has subscriptions — aligns end date to their existing renewal date so they don't get multiple renewal dates.
Why Calendar Month? Selecting Calendar Month aligns the subscription end date to the last day of the month (e.g. 30/04/2027). Without this, the term defaults to exactly 12 months from today and may fall mid-month — causing billing misalignment.

Close the panel. The term end date will now display on the line item in the cart confirming it has been applied.

Quick reference — Term option values explained
Calendar Month  Use this
Aligns end date to the last day of the calendar month. Best for consistent billing across all subscriptions.
Coterminate  Use when adding a new SKU
Use this when adding a brand new license type to a customer who already has subscriptions. Aligns the end date to an existing subscription so the customer doesn't end up with multiple different renewal dates. Keeps everything on one date.
Full term  Do not use
End date falls exactly 12 months from the order date — will land mid-month and cause billing misalignment. Never select this.
